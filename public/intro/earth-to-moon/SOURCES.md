# Earth to Moon

Open index.html in a modern browser. The flyby plays automatically once, then holds the camera at the Moon. Drag the Moon or focus the scene and use the arrow keys to rotate it. Rotation requires WebGL. Reload to replay it. No network connection is needed after extracting the ZIP.

- Earth surface — NASA Earth Observatory, [Blue Marble Next Generation with Topography and Bathymetry](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-topography-bathymetry/), July 2004. Produced by Reto Stöckli, NASA Goddard Space Flight Center.
- Earth clouds — NASA Earth Observatory, [Blue Marble cloud composite](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/the-blue-marble-true-color-global-imagery-at-1km-resolution/), Reto Stöckli and Robert Simmon. The bundled grayscale mask was derived from [this mirror of NASA's cloud layer](https://github.com/MaxwellLee/physics-lab/blob/main/assets/textures/SOURCES.md).
- Moon surface — user-supplied LRO_WAC_Mosaic_Global_303ppd_v02.tif from the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter Camera Wide Angle Camera. The supplied global raster was averaged down to 4096 × 2048 and tone-adjusted for display. Zero-valued samples (about 0.06%) were filled from the NASA SVS lunar map.
- Moon elevation — NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, [CGI Moon Kit](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/), based on Lunar Orbiter Laser Altimeter measurements.

The camera path, timing, lighting, and apparent separation are composed for the animation. These maps are archived satellite datasets.
